# Visual direction

Editorial evidence reel: the interface is the document, not a decorative mockup.
The 900px product viewport and steady 180px caption band form the fixed page.
Camera moves are sparse and only enlarge the operation, boundary, exact proposal,
or WebMCP result currently being discussed.

Use the serif voice for the wordmark and narration captions. Use the quiet sans
voice only for actor and proof labels. Labels form a quiet metadata row inside
the persistent lower band: a short leading rule and actor at left, with the
current tool result or proof aligned right. They never compete with the captured
application or float over product controls.

The caption band is visible for the full film so the lower edge never jumps. Its
upper row holds metadata; narration sits below it. Captions use one or two lines,
generous side margins, and one muted brick underline under the key phrase. Intro
and outro are still, deliberate compositions with no generic particles,
gradients, glass cards or ambient motion.
